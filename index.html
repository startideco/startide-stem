About Startide

Startide STEM

Build it. Test it. Improve it.

Startide STEM is designed as a small, highly interactive after-school program for students in grades 3–8.

The first cohort is intentionally limited to approximately 5–10 students so learners can receive meaningful guidance and spend more time doing.

🤖

Robotics

Program robots to move, respond, navigate, and complete structured challenges.

⌨️

Coding

Develop logic, sequencing, persistence, and computational thinking.

🛠️

Engineering

Design, test, troubleshoot, and improve solutions through iteration.

🧠

Problem-Solving

Break complex challenges into smaller parts and explain how solutions work.
