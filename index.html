Startide STEM Club

Build it. Test it. Improve it.

A highly interactive after-school experience for students in grades 3–8.

Robotics

Program robots to move, respond, navigate and complete challenges.

Coding

Develop logic, sequencing, persistence and computational thinking.

Engineering

Design, test, troubleshoot and improve solutions.

Systems Thinking

Understand how parts connect and why systems behave as they do.

What parents can expect

The intended outcome is bigger than learning one app

The deeper goal is to help students become more capable, curious and resilient thinkers.

Confidence with technology

Approach unfamiliar tools without assuming they are beyond reach.

Persistence through difficulty

Treat a failed first attempt as information, not defeat.

Clearer problem-solving

Break a challenge into smaller, testable parts.

Stronger explanation

Describe what was built, why it works and what could improve.

Responsible teamwork

Share tools, listen to others and contribute to a working group.

A wider view of STEM

See STEM as a way to understand and shape the world.

For families considering Carden because of STEM:

The intention is to offer a serious, evolving program rather than a one-time equipment demonstration. The first cohort will help shape the long-term curriculum.

MAEOS

AI systems that preserve context, judgment and provenance
